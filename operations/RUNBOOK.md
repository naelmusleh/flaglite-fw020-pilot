# FLAGLITE-RUNBOOK-001 — FlagLite Governance Pilot (Synthetic) operations and recovery runbook

## Ownership and escalation

Name the accountable operator, support path, backup contact/role, and escalation criteria. Do not publish private contact details in a public artifact.

## Service overview and dependencies

Link architecture, environments, third-party dependencies, data classifications, and user-facing availability promises.

## Monitoring and alert response

For each signal, record meaning, source, threshold rationale, owner, response window, and false-positive/coverage limits. Do not invent service-level promises.

## Common failure and recovery procedures

Document symptoms, safe diagnosis, containment, retry/rollback, data integrity checks, user communication path, and evidence to preserve. State actions requiring authorization.

## Incident record and learning

Link incident records; record impact, timeline, response, root-cause confidence, corrective tasks, and review date. Preserve uncertainty rather than asserting cause without evidence.

## Data retention, export, and deletion

Link approved policy and actual recovery procedures. Record open decisions; do not imply an unimplemented export/deletion path.
