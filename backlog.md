## Status and priority rules

Define project status vocabulary, priority meaning, and who may authorize scope changes. A task blocked on an owner decision or access must name that dependency and must not be silently substituted.

## Epics and tasks

Each task uses a stable ID (`APP-TASK-###`) and includes: outcome, linked requirement/design/decision IDs, dependencies, owner/agent, scope/exclusions, observable acceptance criteria, verification evidence, status, and follow-up link. Avoid copying the entire specification into tasks.

## Release milestones

Group tasks by reviewable outcome and lifecycle gate. Mark milestone evidence and remaining blockers. Do not label a milestone complete while a required gate is failed.

## Deferred and rejected work

Record material deferrals/rejections and their rationale, decision owner, and reconsideration trigger.
