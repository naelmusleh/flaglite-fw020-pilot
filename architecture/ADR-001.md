# FLAGLITE-ADR-001 — <Architecture decision>

## Context

State the requirements, constraints, evidence, and decision deadline.

## Decision drivers and options

Compare only viable options against identified requirements. Identify unknown cost, security, operational, provider, and migration impacts.

## Decision and status

Record approved choice and accountable approver, or label it a recommendation/open decision. Do not commit to a provider or stack without evidence and approval where required.

## Consequences and controls

Record benefits, risks, failure modes, mitigations, rollback/migration, and observability needs.

## Verification and revisit trigger

Define what evidence will validate the decision and when to reconsider it.

## References

Link requirements, feasibility evidence, reviews, implementation tasks, and superseding decisions.
