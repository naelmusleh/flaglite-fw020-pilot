# FLAGLITE-DESIGN-001 — FlagLite Governance Pilot (Synthetic) experience and design contract

## Design purpose and product promise

State the user promise, design objectives, audience needs, and principles. Link these to accepted specification requirements.

## Experience map and active journeys

Identify applicable foundational, access, core-task, conversion, support/exception, and retention journeys. For each, record actor, entry, exit, primary task, success state, cancel/back path, interruptions, recovery, and status (active, exploration, archived).

## Canonical interaction matrix

Maintain the cross-surface interaction matrix in this artifact or at one linked canonical path. If it becomes a separate artifact, assign it a stable ID and add it to this artifact's `related-artifacts`. For each applicable capability, record web/iOS/Android behavior, equivalent user outcome, intentional difference, accessibility/input needs, requirements, and evidence. Do not keep another independent hand-edited matrix copy.

## Information architecture and navigation

Describe public/private boundaries, primary navigation, deep links, return/continuation, and platform-specific conventions.

## Content, state, and trust contract

Define the meaning of statuses, examples, estimates, unknowns, stale data, errors, confidence, and success feedback. State how sample/demo content is distinguished from live data.

## Brand and design system

Link the approved brand kit and record tokens, typography, components, interaction patterns, and design rationale. Record which platform guidance is adopted and any deliberate exceptions.

## Visual prototypes and evidence

Link Figma or other visual artifacts, name their active source pages/flows, record review scope/date, and state what the prototype does not prove (for example runtime persistence or device accessibility).

## Design decisions and unresolved items

Link decision IDs and CX review findings. An unresolved mandatory design gate remains failed.
